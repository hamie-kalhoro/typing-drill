Daily routine rules

About 20–25 minutes a day. Consistency beats long sessions.

  1. Posture first: back straight, wrists floating, fingers on A S D F and J K L ;.
  2. Never look at the keyboard. Slow and blind beats fast and looking.
  3. Accuracy before speed: if accuracy drops below the week's target, slow down.
  4. Punctuation counts: learn where " ! ? ' ; : live without looking.
  5. Day 7 is checkpoint day: a timed test against the week's target. Missed it? Repeat the week.
